# The casting rubric

This is the part only you can author. It's what separates this skill from an RSS reader. Edit it as your taste sharpens (the TASTE LOG in the Tier 0 prompt and [`rolodex/taste-log.md`](rolodex/taste-log.md) feed changes back into here).

A castable GitHub story usually has these. Score each candidate **1–5** on every dimension, then give an overall score.

## Dimensions

1. **Protagonist**: Is there a clear person or tiny team? A face, not a logo. An individual or a small team, not a faceless org or a pure dependency library.
2. **Visible hook**: Does the project film well? Something you can show: visual, solves a human problem, or delightfully weird. Not an invisible dependency.
3. **Why now**: Is there a reason to tell it this week? Just launched, just went viral, shipped after years of work, hit a real milestone.
4. **Voice**: Evidence they can talk: a real README, a thread, a blog post, a talk. This is your proxy for on-camera presence.
5. **Arc**: Stakes, an underdog angle, an obstacle overcome. Story, not a spec sheet.
6. **Reach**: Is there a plausible contact path? A GitHub profile, a site, a handle. Plus enough variety across geography, background, and project type that your reports don't all look the same.

## Scoring guide

- **5**: Unmistakable. You'd fight to make this one.
- **4**: Strong. A clear yes with one soft spot.
- **3**: Plausible. Could work with the right angle; park it if the week is crowded.
- **2**: Weak on this dimension. Needs something else to carry it.
- **1**: Absent. If it's the protagonist or hook dimension, that's usually a cut.

The overall score is a judgment call, not an average. A 5 on protagonist and hook with a 2 on "why now" can still be a parking-lot maybe. A 2 on protagonist is almost always a cut, however strong the project.

## Exclusions (skip these)

- Big-company / corporate launches with no individual at the center.
- Pure libraries, SDKs, or infra with no human story or nothing to show.
- Anyone already widely covered. Don't bring the obvious viral name everyone has.
- Anyone on the do-not-resurface list in [`rolodex/do-not-resurface.md`](rolodex/do-not-resurface.md).
- Anything matching the current "hard nos" in the Tier 0 prompt's TUNING section.
